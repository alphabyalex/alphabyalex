## Moonshift ($1,500)

Winner of the Best Use of Quicknode bounty at the XRPL Commons AI & Fintech Hackathon (New York City, October 2026), Lending & Borrowing track. The prize was split between two winners, with a $1,500 prize. Built with Tharun Ekambaram, Lucy Zhang, and Kristian Bridge.

Moonshift lets you own a share of a GPU and borrow against it, on the XRP Ledger. Each GPU is split into shares that trade on the XRPL DEX, and shareholders earn USDC payouts from the compute revenue. Shareholders can lock shares as collateral and borrow USDC from a lending vault, with margin calls and liquidations handled onchain. Risk is priced from a borrower's wallet history, read live through Quicknode, combined with the GPU's real revenue, and Claude on Amazon Bedrock writes a plain-English credit memo checked against the data. Built on XRPL devnet. I directed the build, merged the team's work, ran end-to-end testing, and shipped the production demo in 26 hours.

Live app: moonshift-protocol.vercel.app  
Repo: github.com/alphabyalex/XRP-Hackathon-2026

## Fluxline ($30,000)

1st place, Sustainability Track, and 1 of 3 overall winners at East v West Hackathon 2026, out of 19 teams. $30,000 prize Judged by OpenAI, Google, AWS, Anthropic, Sequoia Capital, Lovable, Lightspeed Ventures, and Underscore VC.

Fluxline prices the risk of flexible grid interconnection for data centers. It tells a company how often they'd realistically get cut off under a flexible connection, and whether that's actually worth it compared to waiting years for a normal one. Every number is either backed by real public grid data or clearly labeled as an assumption. Built with my team, Optica.

Repo: github.com/alphabyalex/fluxline-hackathon

## Senthos ($7000)

1st place at the USC SCBC Hackathon 2026, $7,000 prize, judged by the Solana Foundation. 

Senthos is a structured products protocol on Solana that tokenizes live prediction market positions. It bundles 100+ Polymarket contracts into tradeable basket tokens with tranched risk slices, so a user can express a view with a clear risk profile instead of picking individual markets one at a time. I led the frontend and product architecture.

Repo: https://github.com/LuKresXD/SCBC-Hackathon-2026

## IPO Alpha Engine

A quantitative research tool for evaluating IPOs before they list, using only information available pre-IPO: deal structure and underwriter signals, S-1 financials, and probability based outcome estimates. Covers both upcoming IPOs and a backtest of historical ones going back to 2020.

Live app: alpha-engine-research.com  
Repo: github.com/alphabyalex/IPO_Alpha_Engine

## Contra

Highest Ranked Track 2 (Prediction markets), of the Artemis Quant Research Competition.

Contra is a Solana native prediction market protocol that identifies mispriced longshot bets on Kalshi and Polymarket, where a market priced at 8% might only resolve YES 1.7% of the time, and packages that edge into tradeable baskets with optional leverage.

Repo: github.com/alphabyalex/Contra
